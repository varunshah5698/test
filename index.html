03About

A practice of small corrections.

Akshar means letter. The studio is one person, a scanner, and a drawer of proofs that refuses to close.

Shiv Patel is a type designer and lettering artist based in Ahmedabad. He draws text faces for long reading and sign systems for short glances, and runs the studio Akshar Type.

He learned to letter in a sign painter’s workshop in Kalupur, spent four years at a type foundry in Mumbai, and has worked independently since 2019. He teaches a monthly lettering workshop at Studio Chaap and keeps the shopfront archive that feeds most of his drawings.

Currently Drawing a Gujarati companion for Marginalia, due 2027.
